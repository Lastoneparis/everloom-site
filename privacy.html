Private by design

Everloom Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 July 2026

The short version: Everloom does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of your data. Your photos and everything Everloom does with them stay on your device. We don’t run servers that hold your content, and we have no way to see your photos.

1. Data we collect

None. Everloom has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking SDKs. We do not collect personal information, usage data, device identifiers, or location.

2. Your photos

Everloom accesses your photo library only to display, organize, and search your gallery. All processing — including AI content search and on-device face grouping — happens entirely on your device using Apple’s frameworks. Your photos are never uploaded to us and never leave your device through Everloom.

3. iCloud backup (optional)

If you choose to back up, Everloom uses your own iCloud/Apple Account storage. This is a service between you and Apple, governed by Apple’s privacy policy. We operate no servers and have no access to anything you back up.

4. Purchases & subscriptions

Everloom Premium is sold through Apple’s App Store. Payments and subscription management are handled entirely by Apple. We never receive or store your payment details. See Apple’s Terms of Use (EULA): apple.com/legal/…/stdeula.

5. Third parties

Everloom includes no third-party analytics, advertising, or data-broker SDKs. The only external service is Apple’s App Store (for purchases) and, if you enable it, your own iCloud.

6. Children

Everloom is rated 4+ and collects no data from anyone, including children.

7. Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, export, or delete on our side. Photos are managed entirely by you in the Apple Photos system and your own iCloud. You can revoke Everloom’s photo access at any time in your device Settings.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the “Last updated” date above.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email hugomoriceau@icloud.com.
