Privacy Policy

Everloom

Last updated 17 September 2026 · Data controller: Oshi Lab, Switzerland · Applies to Everloom on iPhone, iPad and Mac

The short version

Everloom has no servers, no account and no sign-in. Your photos are never uploaded, and I never receive any personal data from this app. Everything the app does with your pictures — the timeline, the search index, the face grouping — is computed and stored on the device you are holding. The one exception is described below: with Premium, your album names can be backed up to your own iCloud, never to me.

Photo library access

The app asks the system for access to your photo library, because a photo app without photos is of no use. That access is used to display your library, to build a search index on the device, and to write back a photo you choose to save. It is never used to transmit an image anywhere, because there is nowhere to transmit it to. You can withdraw or narrow the permission at any time in the system privacy settings; the app will then show only what you still allow.

Face recognition

Faces are detected and grouped using Apple's on-device Vision and Core ML frameworks. The resulting face data is biometric data about you and the people in your photos, and it is treated accordingly:

  • It is computed on your device and written only to the app's own storage on that device.
  • It is never transmitted, never synchronised, never shared with me or with any third party, and never matched against any external database.
  • It is not used to identify anyone by name unless you yourself name a group, and that name stays local too.
  • Deleting the app deletes all of it. Turning face grouping off in the app's settings removes the groupings it built.

Because none of it leaves the device, I am not in a position to disclose it, lose it or be compelled to hand it over.

Album backup to your own iCloud (Premium)

Premium includes an album backup, and it is the one feature that writes anything off the device — so it is described exactly. When it is on, Everloom stores your album names and, for each album, Apple's cloud identifiers for the photos in it in your own iCloud key-value store, under your own Apple Account.

  • No photograph is uploaded — a cloud identifier is a reference to a picture that already lives in your iCloud Photos, not the picture.
  • It goes to your iCloud, not to any server of mine. I have no account with which to read it, and Apple's key-value store is private to you.
  • It is what makes an album you built on the iPhone appear on the Mac.
  • Apple's own privacy policy governs iCloud. Turning off iCloud for Everloom in your system settings stops it; so does not subscribing.

The search index

Content search works by reading your photos with Apple's on-device vision models and storing keywords next to them locally. The index is an ordinary file in the app's storage. It never leaves the device and is included in an encrypted device backup if you make one.

Purchases

Everloom Premium is sold and processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. I never see your name, payment method or Apple Account. The app asks Apple's StoreKit whether an entitlement is active on this device and receives yes or no.

Analytics and advertising

There are none. No advertising SDK, no crash reporter, no attribution SDK, no telemetry, no third-party analytics library. Everloom does not ask for the App Tracking Transparency permission because it has nothing to track. I do not know how many photos you have, whether the index finished, or whether you still use the app.

Children

Everloom is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone of any age.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection over personal data a controller holds about you. I hold none from this app, so there is nothing for me to disclose, correct or erase. Every piece of data described above is on your own device and under your own control: deleting the app removes all of it permanently. If you would like this confirmed in writing, write to contact@oshi-messenger.com and I will answer within thirty days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it, and the new text is published here before the change ships in an app update.
