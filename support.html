Support

Everloom holds nothing about you, so tell me everything.

There is no account and no server, which means I cannot look at your library, reproduce your index or read a log. What you write is all the evidence there is — please be generous with it.

contact@oshi-messenger.com

Include your device and OS version, roughly how many photos are in your library, and what you did before it went wrong. Do not send me photos — I do not need them and would rather not have them.

Indexing

It has been indexing for a long time.
The first pass reads every photo with the on-device vision models, and on a library of tens of thousands that is genuinely long. Leave the app open, plug the device in, and let the screen stay on. iOS throttles this work aggressively when the battery is low or the device is hot, so a warm phone can appear to stall. It only ever happens once.
It restarts from zero.
Progress is saved as it goes, so a restart from zero is a bug. Tell me the OS version and whether the app was force-quit or the device ran out of storage.
Can I stop it?
Yes — close the app. It resumes where it stopped. The gallery and albums work throughout; only content search waits for the index.

Search and faces

Search finds nothing.
If the index is incomplete, search covers only what has been read so far. Once it has finished, try a plainer word: the models recognise objects, scenes and places, not names or moods. "dog" works; "Rex" cannot.
Two people are grouped as one, or one person appears twice.
That happens with siblings, with big gaps in age, and with sunglasses. Merge or split the group by hand and the correction sticks. The grouping never leaves the device, so there is no shared model learning from it either.
Are my faces sent anywhere?
No. Face data is computed and stored on the device only. There is no server to send it to. See the privacy policy.

Photos and storage

Some photos are blank or will not open.
Usually iCloud Photos: the full-size file is still in the cloud and the device only has a thumbnail. Turn on Download and Keep Originals, or let iCloud fetch them while you are on Wi-Fi.
Does Everloom take up extra space?
The index and thumbnails do, but it is small relative to the photos themselves. Everloom never duplicates your originals.
If I delete a photo in Everloom, is it gone?
It goes to the system Recently Deleted like any other deletion, and can be recovered there for thirty days. Everloom is not a backup — keep one.
Why can't I see my iPhone albums on the Mac?
Because there is no account and nothing syncs. That is a deliberate trade for having no server, and it is the one thing people most often wish were otherwise. If it matters to you, say so — it is the feature I am most often asked for.
