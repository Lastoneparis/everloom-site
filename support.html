Frequently asked

Where are my photos stored? On your device. Everloom never uploads them. Optional backup uses your own iCloud.

How does search work offline? Everloom understands your photos on-device using Apple’s frameworks — no internet needed and nothing is sent anywhere.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription? In the Settings app → your name → Subscriptions, or via Everloom → Settings → Manage subscription.

How do I restore a purchase? Everloom → Settings → Restore purchases.

Do you have a promo code? Everloom → Settings → Redeem a code.
