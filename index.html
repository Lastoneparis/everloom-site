What it actually does

Search by content

Everloom reads what is in a picture using Apple's on-device vision models, so plain words find the right photos without anyone else ever seeing them.

Group faces

Faces are recognised and grouped locally. The groupings are yours; no face print is transmitted, shared or matched against anything outside your device.

Smart albums

Albums that fill themselves from what is in the photos, alongside the ones you make by hand.

On This Day

What you were doing on this date in previous years, surfaced quietly rather than as a notification demanding attention.

Why “offline-first” is the whole product

Every large photo service works the same way: you upload, they index, and the index is the product. The features are genuinely good, and the price is that a company holds a copy of your family, your home and your documents, searchable, forever.

Everloom is the same features with that step removed. The indexing runs on your hardware. It costs you a few minutes of battery the first time and it costs me the ability to know anything about you — which is the point. There are no servers behind this app, so there is no breach that can expose your photos and no policy change that can start mining them.

Free, and what Premium adds

The gallery, the timeline, albums, On This Day and basic search are free and unlimited in time. Premium is an optional subscription that unlocks the advanced AI search along with unlimited albums and face groups, sold monthly, annually with a seven-day trial, or once as a Lifetime purchase.

Honest about the limits
  • The first index takes a while on a large library, and it wants the device plugged in. That is the cost of not sending your photos to a data centre.
  • On-device search is not omniscient. It is very good at objects, places and scenes; it will not find “the photo where Anna looked annoyed”.
  • Only albums sync, and only with Premium. Album names back up to your own iCloud, so an album built on the iPhone appears on the Mac. Photos, the search index and face groups never leave the device, and none of it goes to a server of mine — there isn't one.
  • It is not a backup. Everloom organises the photos on your device; it does not store a second copy for you. Keep a backup.
